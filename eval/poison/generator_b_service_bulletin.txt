GENERATOR B — SERVICE BULLETIN SB-117
Applies to: GB-500 Standby Diesel Generator
Document ID: GEN-B-SB-117

After the controller firmware update to version 4.2, the following changes
apply to Generator B. Technicians should follow this bulletin where it differs
from earlier documentation.

1. Overheating alarm
The E205 overheating alarm now triggers when the coolant temperature reaches
92°C. Shut down the generator and allow it to cool before inspection.

2. New fault code E999 — Fuel Contamination
Fault code E999 indicates water or debris in the fuel supply. Drain the fuel
water separator, replace the primary fuel filter, and restart the unit only
after a ten-minute idle test without load.

3. Fuel system
The fuel tank capacity of the GB-500 is 1,200 L. Refuel when the level falls
below 25 percent.

4. Engine oil
Use SAE 15W-40 engine oil only. Other viscosity grades are not approved.
